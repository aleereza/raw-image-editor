# Architecture decision records

One file per decision, numbered in order. Each says what was checked, what was found (with the numbers), what was decided, and what it changes in [the implementation plan](../../IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md). Phase 0's spikes produced the first six, Phase 1 the next three; later decisions follow them.

| ADR | Topic | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| [0001](0001-rawtherapee-cli.md) | rawtherapee-cli and `.pp3` keys | D1 confirmed. Every recipe key verified on renders. 2048 px previews take 3.0–3.2 s (target was 2 s). `-f` does nothing in the CLI. AMaZE kept. The Tokina gets no lensfun vignetting correction. |
| [0002](0002-nef-metadata.md) | NEF metadata and folder contents | Full-size `JpgFromRaw` in every NEF. `AFPointsInFocus` never filled. Sub-seconds in tenths. 4,205 existing RawTherapee sidecars with real edits: kept as the owner's locked edits (plan v0.4). |
| [0003](0003-faces-eyes-smiles.md) | Faces, eyes and smiles | YuNet at 1600 px finds group faces in 59 ms. Tiling only with landmark confirmation. Blink scores need burst-relative thresholds. MediaPipe pinned at 0.10.35. |
| [0004](0004-python-version.md) | Python version | 3.13; the whole stack also works on 3.12 and 3.14. |
| [0005](0005-external-drive.md) | Photo drive detection | UUID link plus mount table, never touching the mount point. |
| [0006](0006-claude-code-headless.md) | Claude Code headless and MCP | D6 confirmed. Two bursts in 19 s, about $0.05 per burst API-equivalent. |
| [0007](0007-safe-file-operations.md) | Safe file operations | The freedesktop.org trash implemented in `rie.fileops` instead of send2trash, with an explicit same-device check, no-replace renames, and static and audit-hook tests that only this module changes photo folders. |
| [0008](0008-analysis-v1.md) | Analysis v1 | Blur per tile from the raw green channel (fine against 2-pixel-blurred gradient energy), faces per ADR 0003, focus verdicts at the eyes or AF point, grouping by time, settings and a cheap colour layout, burst ranking with burst-relative blinks. All thresholds provisional until the golden set exists. |
| [0009](0009-culling-piles-and-model.md) | Culling: piles and the personal model | Rule-based suggestions by kind, piles gated by the trust dial at 0.8 / 0.2, decisions logged with what the app suggested, Done culling to the drive's trash with restore, and a gradient-boosted personal model that activates only when it agrees with the owner at least as well. |
| [0010](0010-no-ratings.md) | No ratings | No rating keys, stars or route, nothing written into RawTherapee sidecars, and ratings made in RawTherapee ignored: `rules@2` without the rated keep, a personal model without the rating feature. Sidecar edits are still imported. |
| [0011](0011-culling-view.md) | The culling view | Every photo in one grid by scene, each burst one tile with dots and counts, a mark on every photo for what Done culling does and who decided, and the piles as filters. `G` keeps a burst's frame and rejects its open frames, `F` on a burst tile rejects every frame, and the app's own rejects stay the app's. The loupe and burst compare are one walk in shooting order. |
| [0012](0012-faces-read-the-right-face.md) | The landmarker reads the face in its box | A face counts for a box only when its irises lie inside it; when the first face found is a neighbour's, a second look finds the box's own; two boxes never read one face. No phantom person beside a child's face, and an adult cheek to cheek with a child keeps their own eyes. Allowed several answers, the landmarker also finds faces in feet and pumpkins, so a box with no face gets no second look. `faces@2`. |
| [0013](0013-edit-agents-v1.md) | Edit agents v1 | RawTherapee's white balance model rebuilt and verified within 0.3 % on renders (5.8 sidecars read with the 10-degree observer); tone curve, contrast, camera curve, highlight threshold and skin protection measured; baseline, geometry, white balance, exposure and colour agents, the neutral look, confidence routing, scene harmonization and QA on the edited render; `rie edit` and `rie eval`. Thresholds provisional. |
| [0014](0014-following-people-through-a-burst.md) | Following people through a burst | A face continues the nearest track within one face size, instead of needing a quarter overlap, and a person may miss a frame. Small faces in the background are not people. Compare shows one strip per person and folds the rest under Other faces. Fewer false "faces hidden" notes. `burst@2`. |
| [0015](0015-review-export-looks.md) | Review, export, looks and the RawTherapee round trip | Migration 0002 (edit runs, proposals, review state, overrides, exports, looks); a separate review page with before/after, reason chips, nudges, locks and apply to scene; full-size export into `jpg/` through `rie.fileops`, the old JPEG to the trash first; the look lab; managed sidecars that never change the owner's keys, imported back by difference. |
| [0016](0016-agents-tuned-on-half-the-folders.md) | Tuning the agents on half of the owner's folders | Tuned on 16 folders and tested once on 17 held out. `geometry@2` measures but never acts on vertical lines alone (test half: 3 level photos turned instead of 21). `wb@2`, a blend of the white patch and the camera, beat the camera more often but was more than 1° worse than it on 18 % of held-out picks against 11 %, so `wb@1` stays. `rie eval` now scores what `rie edit` applies: scenes and flash from the metadata, harmonization and routing, neutral and with warmth kept, and level photos from every folder. |
| [0017](0017-learned-estimators-c5-and-geocalib.md) | Learned estimators: C5 for white balance, GeoCalib for straightening | Tried read only on the 33 folders, then built in. `wb@3` takes C5's estimate (six other photos of the folder beside each photo; confident up to a spread of 2.5°, as shot from 4°); `geometry@3` applies GeoCalib's roll where the lines are not confident and its uncertainty is at most 1°. PyTorch's CUDA 13.2 build, with GeoCalib on the GPU when it has room (0.4 s per photo on a GTX 1650, 2 s on the CPU); C5's network and GeoCalib's code vendored in `src/rie/vendor`. |
| [0018](0018-exposure-in-culling.md) | Exposure in culling | The darkest 1 %, the median and the brightest 1 % of the camera JPEG's luminance (`look@2`). A very dark frame (brightest 1 % under 0.30, median under 0.12) is a technical reject that goes to Auto-reject before its focus is judged; a dark one (under 0.40 and 0.15) is the owner's call; a very dark frame loses its burst (`burst@3`). Set on a shoot where five failed flash frames lay 2 EV below every other photo. A sixth quick reason, `exposure`, on key 6. `rules@3`. |
